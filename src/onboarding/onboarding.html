Key Features

Auto Tab Switcher is packed with features to make your browsing experience seamless.

⌨️

Keyboard Shortcut

Press Ctrl+Shift+P (or Cmd+Shift+P on Mac) to instantly pause/resume without opening the popup

⏸️

Pause on Activity

Automatically pauses when you interact with tabs to avoid interrupting your work

🎨

Visual Themes

Distinct color themes for Global and Window modes help you stay oriented

⚙️

Flexible Settings

Customize intervals, pause duration, and startup behavior to match your workflow

🚀

You're All Set!

Ready to start automatically switching tabs? Click the extension icon in your toolbar to begin.

1 Click the extension icon in your toolbar
2 Choose Global or Window mode
3 Click "Enable" to start auto-switching
⌨️ Use Ctrl+Shift+P to pause/resume anytime

💡 Pro Tip: Visit Settings to customize the keyboard shortcut and other features!
